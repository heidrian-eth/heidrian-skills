Rail

The month is one rail. What is still open sits above today, what is settled sits below, and the one thing that needs you appears on the rail ahead of you.

1 · Brief

Case. Adults checking several times a day, one-handed, often in public, for 5–10 seconds. No brand assets given: one green-black brand ink assumed. Platform navigation untouched. AA contrast, tabular figures, no ambiguous figures.

Content truth. Money moves through a month: a past you cannot change, a now, and a short future you can still affect.

Product-wide problems (crux first): 1 urgent without alarm · 2 one figure wins the glance · 3 trust: exact and still · 4 dense records stay scannable, in vs out without red/green · 5 position in time · 6 actions under the thumb.

3 · Principles

K1 One rail per screen; everything hangs from it. K2 One key figure per screen. K3 Open above, settled below. K4 One signal, and only for the future. K5 The base carries the actions.

5 · Type, line, shape

T1 One variable grotesque (Instrument Sans), tabular figures. T2 Two sizes: 14/20 and 56/56; controls 16/20. T3 Otherwise weight (300, 400, 600) and position against the rail.

F1 Two line weights: 1px and 4px. F2 No cards, boxes, shadows or rounded corners. Focus: 2px ink ring, 2px offset. F3 Markers on the rail: filled = in or done, hollow = out or pending; 5 / 8 / 13px by amount band (a kit choice).

6 · Motion and interaction

M1 The user's actions are ink: the rail draws (360ms linear), rows appear as it passes (80ms stagger), press is a 120ms value step. M2 The system's news is light: the signal fades in over 800ms, last. M3 Nothing translates, scales, bounces or counts up. I1 Press = one value step. I2 A row is one target ≥ 52px. I3 The one unfamiliar control: press and hold the key figure to cover it.

7 · Structure and components

S1 Rail: a 1px ink line at x = 64, the only vertical. Label column x 16–52 flush right; everything else hangs at x = 80; amounts flush right. S2 Row 52 · Ahead slot 120 · base 56 · nav 48 · spacing 4/8/12/16/28. S3 Every screen: Head → Fields → Ahead → Edge → Settled → Base (zones may be absent, order never changes). S4 The edge is a ground step.

Components: Rail · Marker · Key figure · Row · Field · Ahead slot (clear / watch / over; never collapses) · Edge · Base bar · Nav (v1.1) · Notice (fault) · Empty · Loading.

8 · Voice

V1 Forecasts are dated and quantified, future tense: "runs out Tue 24". V2 Records are past tense and bare. V3 No exclamation marks, no "Warning", no praise. Amounts always carry currency and two decimals.

9 · Exception, Do / Don't

X1 The signal segment is the only thick, coloured, slow thing in a hairline, achromatic, quick system. One per screen.

Do hang every text from x = 80 · keep the Ahead slot when it has nothing to say. Don't add a card, shadow, merchant logo or avatar ring · use a third type size · colour an amount · use the signal for promotion · use primaries with black rules, cream paper or a rotated label · animate a figure.
