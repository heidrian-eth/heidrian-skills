# Changelog: visual check of the 50 artist entries

Every entry was first written from museum and scholarly texts only. This log records what changed after the key works were downloaded from open-access sources, looked at, and colour-sampled (k-means in CIELAB on the painted/built area with frames, mats and gallery walls cropped out; the hex is the median real pixel of each cluster, the % is its area share).

Format: `Artist: what changed (why, from which work)`.


## Bauhaus
- Albers: nested-square offsets confirmed by pixel measurement on four AIC scans: every inward step is 1u at the sides, 0.5u below, 1.5u above (bottom:top 1:3, error ≤0.07u). Yellow Signal 1962: rings 681/514 px in an 843 px field, margins 0.93/1.0u sides, 0.46u bottom, 1.44u top; Promise 1967 and New Light 1967 agree.
- Albers: "10/8/6/4" corrected. Three-square works are 10/8/6 (Yellow Signal, Promise) or 10/6/4 (New Light, the 8 ring omitted); the four-square Light Passage 1956 measures 9/7/5/3 (807/625/446/263 px, u = 90 px), not 10/8/6/4.
- Albers: "make the most important element the least saturated" reversed. In all four checked works the core is the most saturated field (Yellow Signal core C*84 vs rings 59–62; Light Passage orange core C*70 vs grey outer C*7) or the lightest (New Light). With Rays (the grey-core source of the old rule) could not be obtained.
- Albers: "single-hue ladder" widened to "one hue family, or a neutral outer field stepping to one saturated core" (Light Passage: grey → ochre → olive → orange). Added: adjacent outer rings differ by only 3–5 L* (Yellow Signal L*83 → 86), measured area shares (10/8/6: 36/28/36%; 9/7/5/3: 40/30/20/11%), hard straight edges with slightly mottled fills.
